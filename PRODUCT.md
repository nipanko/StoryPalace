# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

Desktop app, Windows (.exe) and macOS (.app). Not a website; mouse + keyboard first.

## Stack

Godot 4 (user's choice over Electron and Rust/Tauri). Users switch between a 2D view and a 3D view of the same palace, by preference.

## Users

Everyone from teens to adults: students preparing for exams, professionals, lifelong learners. Many have never heard of the memory palace (method of loci) and are not gamers, so nothing may assume prior knowledge or game controls.

## Product Purpose

Help people remember anything by building memory palaces: pick or photograph familiar places, put vivid images at spots in them along a numbered path, write the little story that links them, then walk the path and test recall. Success is a person who can recite a list, a speech, a concept or a story from memory after building and walking one palace.

## Positioning

The method of loci made hands-on: the place, the images, the path and the recall test live in one tool, in 2D or a walkable 3D view, offline on the person's own computer.

## Operating Context

- Build: create a palace, add rooms (ready-made illustrated rooms or a photo of a real place), click spots to place memories (emoji or own picture + what to remember + the story), order them into a path.
- Walk: guided tour station by station, in 2D or 3D.
- Recall: quiz that shows the place and image, hides the fact, self-graded; tracks last practice and score.
- Data stays local; palaces can be exported and imported as files to share.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Must start fast and stay smooth on ordinary laptops.
- Works fully offline; no account.
- Undecided: cloud sync, smart picture suggestions, spaced-repetition scheduling, sharing service.

## Brand Commitments

- Maker credit, exactly: "Built with 💖 by Storybook Interactive Private Limited".
- Credit only the maker; no other names or attributions in the app, repo, commits or docs.
- Product name: StoryPalace.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No testimonials, user counts or studies exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Natural before clever: a first-timer builds a working palace without reading instructions.
2. The palace is the content: the user's places and images lead; chrome stays out of the way.
3. Vivid and personal: every station invites a picture and a story, not just a fact.
4. Local and theirs: nothing leaves the computer unless the user exports it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable type sizes, full keyboard flow for walk and recall (arrows, space), high contrast text, no reliance on colour alone for right/wrong.
